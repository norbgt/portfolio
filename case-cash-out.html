# Cash-out experience

Overview

XP Inc ↗ Service & Concept Design 2025

What we did

A new mobile cash-out experience was developed to address critical pain points that accounted for over 40% of fund-related customer inquiries

My role

I led the end-to-end design process while mentoring the team in service design practices, from initial discovery to delivery

Status

In progress

Big numbers

27 pain points 21 new concepts 13 new backlog items 4 initiatives were prioritized for Q3 2025

Workshop & Cocreate

Our team kicked off the blueprint and opportunity mapping in a co-creation workshop

The agenda included the activation, trading, and custody teams, ensuring a complete journey mapping process

Blueprint & Data analysis

We combined user interviews with the analysis of experience metrics like NPS, CSAT, and Contact Rate

Customers struggled most with unclear timelines and not knowing how much they would ultimately receive

Concept & Prioritize

We created over 20 viable design concepts to help shape an ideal end-to-end cash-out experience

The concepts were scheduled for short and medium-term implementation
